Benford's law

Share of first digits of all amounts versus Benford's law. MAD 0.0051 → close conformity.

The ledger as a whole conforms, but split by user one person stands out:

KOCH_J has far too many amounts starting with 4 – the same user who posts many invoices just below the 5,000 € approval limit. In a real audit this would be followed up.
